## SCI-002: explicitly bounded, not silently fixed

The distinct-physical-game sensitivity found **six player/timestamp groups with
twelve distinct player-game rows**, including one row with positive minutes.
These are distinct simultaneous games associated with traded players, separate
from the earlier four duplicated physical-game pairs. A game-ID tie breaker
would invent a temporal order and could leak same-time minutes into another
association. The existing guard was preserved.

Consequently both physical-game sensitivity variants are blocked in both date
phases, with zero accepted sensitivity model results. The SCI-001-only primary
retains the literal historical roster-row workload convention. Defining the
appropriate traded-player observation/history convention remains a scientific
decision; no human medical classification or numerical adjustment was invented.
This limitation does not change the successful SCI-001-only comparison.
