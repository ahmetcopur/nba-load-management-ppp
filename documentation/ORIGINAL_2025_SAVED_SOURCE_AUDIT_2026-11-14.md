## Successful saved-inventory selection and blocking discrepancy

Both full and cadence-harmonized selections independently matched **1,230/1,230 original PDF identities, publication times and pre-tip ages**, using all 5,023 saved candidates and the 507 eligible cadence candidates. Both panels select 422 hourly-source and 808 targeted-source games. The 355 distinct harmonized selected reports are thus derived from the saved candidate inventory rather than assumed from the archived selector.

The original canonical selector function applied to fresh source records, with date-keyed source diagnostics and the existing precedence, reproduced **every archived game-table field**. The date-keyed diagnostic excludes source rows with missing game dates rather than inventing dates; the original 2025 S4 join implementation itself has not been recovered.

**Strict team-table acceptance fails.** In each panel, 1,598 team rows differ in both `team_final_status` and `inferred_no_listed_players`. The archived full table has 1,630 inferred-clean teams versus 32 in the source-based reconstruction; the harmonized table has 1,631 versus 33. Independently of the reconstruction, the archived game and team tables contradict each other's inferred-clean counts for **800 games per panel**. The other compared team fields, including usable submission status, match. No regression effect is asserted or estimated here.

The inspected S4 ZIP contains output tables and reports, not the original team-join program. The canonical builder merely standardized/copied this 2025 table. Library searches recovered the acquisition generator but not that missing join program. Do not label this computational artifact a human-reviewed medical decision, and do not infer an undocumented formula solely to force its flags to match.

[Machine-readable diagnostic evidence](../validation/2025_source_selector_audit_2026-11-14/source_selection_audit.json) explicitly reports `blocked_legacy_team_metadata` and `all_replays_exact: false`. Audit mode writes aggregate diagnostics only; strict generation raises on the mismatch and does not accept/substitute selector CSVs. A targeted 2025 downstream run is therefore not advertised as ready.
